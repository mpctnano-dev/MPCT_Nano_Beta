# By Sample Type

Start from what you have. You do not need to know the method or the machine yet. Each page opens with the material and routes the question to a method and an instrument at the MPaCT Lab in Flagstaff, Arizona.

| You have | Page | Typical first question |
|---|---|---|
| A deposited film on a substrate | [Thin films](thin-films.md) | How thick is it, and how rough? |
| A wafer or a coupon cut from one | [Wafers and coupons](wafers-and-coupons.md) | Pattern it, probe it, or cleave it? |
| A powder, catalyst, or porous solid | [Powders and porous solids](powders-and-porous-solids.md) | What is the surface area and pore size? |
| A bar, pellet, ceramic, or large part | [Bulk solids](bulk-solids.md) | Phase, thermoelectric numbers, or dimensions? |
| A design that needs to become a part | [Prototype parts and fixtures](prototype-parts.md) | Print it, mill it, or turn it? |
| A die, packaged chip, or failed assembly | [Devices and packaged parts](devices-and-packaged-parts.md) | Attach it, wire it, or inspect it? |

If your sample is not listed, describe it to staff. They will route it.
